Validating conversation keywords against attitudes, with a no-chat arm and delayed follow-up. 4 conditions: Op-ed + chatbot, Op-ed only, Placebo + chatbot, Placebo only. Primary outcome: Immediate change in support toward the op-ed position (post minus pre), with the 2-week change as the durability outcome.. Population: US online panel adults who hold a position on government spending, about 400 per arm (1,600 total before attrition).
